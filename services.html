Services

Engineering Disciplines

M&Z Design Consulting delivers practical, cost-effective civil engineering across grading, drainage, earthworks, road design, stormwater management, and Civil 3D services throughout Alberta and British Columbia.

Civil Engineering Design
Comprehensive civil site design from feasibility through IFC drawing packages for any project type and regulatory environment.
  • IFC / IFA / Tender packages
  • Site servicing & utility coordination
  • Technical specifications
  • Construction support services
Grading & Earthworks
Cut/fill analysis, mass haul optimization, and finished grading design to minimize cost and maximize constructability.
  • Cut/fill volume optimization
  • Mass haul & import/export analysis
  • Finished grade & terrain design
  • Earthwork cost reduction strategies
Stormwater Management
Full-spectrum stormwater engineering — hydrologic and hydraulic analysis, SWM pond design, detention systems, and ESC planning.
  • Hydrologic & hydraulic modeling
  • SWM pond & detention design
  • Outlet structures & spillway design
  • IDF studies & flood routing
Road & Access Design
Road geometry design, intersection design, and truck access solutions for industrial, commercial, and residential developments.
  • Industrial & municipal road design
  • Intersection & truck access design
  • Highway geometric design
  • Access management
Culvert & Drainage Design
Hydraulic design of culverts, storm sewers, and drainage structures. Site drainage analysis and infrastructure upgrades.
  • Culvert hydraulic design
  • Storm sewer & drainage design
  • Infrastructure upgrades
  • Site drainage assessments
Civil 3D Services
Advanced Civil 3D modeling and drafting support. Surface modeling, corridor design, quantity calculations, and drawing production.
  • Surface & corridor modeling
  • Quantity calculations & mass haul
  • Drafting support & drawing production
  • 3D grading design
Land Development Engineering
End-to-end civil engineering for land development from feasibility and subdivision layout through municipal approvals.
  • Subdivision & site layout
  • Municipal approvals coordination
  • Servicing & utility design
  • Development constraints analysis
Tank Containment Systems
Secondary containment design, industrial berm engineering, volume capacity calculations for energy and industrial facilities.
  • Secondary containment berm design
  • Volume capacity calculations
  • Spill containment design
  • AB & BC regulatory compliance
Construction Support Services
On-site engineering support, RFI responses, site instruction preparation, and construction inspection services.
  • RFI & site instruction responses
  • Construction inspection support
  • As-built & record drawing production
  • Field engineering support
